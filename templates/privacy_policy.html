Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 Oct 2026

1. Information we collect

We may collect the information needed to process your order or support request, including your name, mobile number, delivery address, order items, total value, payment reference information, and communications with our support team.

We may also store account information if you create an account with us, such as your registered mobile number, name, delivery address, and password hash.

2. How we use your information

We use customer information to process and fulfil orders, confirm delivery, handle support requests, send order and delivery updates, maintain records of transactions, and improve the quality of our store and service.

3. How we protect your information

We store sensitive data in secure server-side environment variables and access it only through the application backend. Access to administrative tools is restricted to authorized staff, and we use strong session controls and password hashing where required.

We use HTTPS in production environments and enforce transport security, secure cookies, and security headers to reduce risk of interception and misuse.

4. Payment and SMS services

Payment processing and SMS notifications are handled through trusted third-party providers. We do not store raw card numbers or card CVV data. Only the minimum payment reference information required for order validation and reconciliation is retained.

5. Cookies and tracking

We use essential cookies to keep the shopping cart, session, and site functionality working correctly. If analytics or marketing cookies are enabled, we will request consent before placing them and only use them in a privacy-conscious and transparent manner.

6. Data retention

We retain order records, customer support records, and transaction references only as long as necessary to provide the services, reconcile payments, comply with legal requirements, and resolve disputes.

7. Your rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, ask for the deletion or limitation of certain data where legally applicable, and ask questions about how your information is processed. To do so, contact the store owner or support team through the information available on the website.

8. Contact

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please use the contact information available on the website or visit the contact section on the home page.
